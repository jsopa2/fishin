# Roadmap

**CURRENT ACTIVE PHASE: V0 — Wisconsin Prediction Feasibility**

Later phases are inactive until V0 is sufficiently validated.

## V0 — Prediction Feasibility

Establish whether Wisconsin public observations and environmental data support
a validated, bounded fishing-conditions benchmark. Other Midwest states and
waterbodies are expansion targets only after the Wisconsin gates pass.

## V1 — Fishing Forecast

Location + species + date/time → forecast.

## V2 — Where Should I Fish?

Maps, water bodies, access points, habitat, and environmental intelligence.

## V3 — Continuous Validation

Fishing-trip logging, catch observations, weather attachment, and prediction-vs-outcome evaluation.

## V4 — Personal Fishing Intelligence

Personalized recommendations based on individual fishing history.
